SMS program and consent

Safe Care Transportation offers SMS messages about marketing and account notifications, including transportation request updates and scheduling communications. By selecting the SMS consent checkbox and submitting your request, you agree to receive these messages at the phone number you provide. The checkbox is optional and is not selected by default. Consent is not a condition of purchasing transportation.

Phone number privacy

Information (Phone Numbers) obtained as part of the SMS consent process will not be shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

For details about information handling, please read our Privacy Policy.

Message frequency and charges

Messaging frequency may vary. Message and data rates may apply according to your wireless plan. Contact your mobile carrier with questions about these charges.

Opt out

Reply STOP to any message to opt out at any time. You may receive a confirmation of your opt-out. To request assistance with opting out, contact safecare70@gmail.com.

Help and support

For help, text 574-390-7688 or email safecare70@gmail.com.

Transportation requests

Submitting a form or sending a text does not confirm a ride. Pickup details, availability, pricing, and any return trip must be confirmed with Safe Care Transportation. This is non-emergency transportation; call 911 for a medical emergency.

Delivery and changes

Message delivery depends on mobile network availability and may be delayed or unavailable. Contact us directly for time-sensitive scheduling questions. Updates to these terms will be posted here with an updated date.
