Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Information you provide

Safe Care Transportation provides the SafeCare Medical Rides website. When you request a ride or contact us, you may provide your name, telephone number, email address, pickup and destination addresses, requested date and time, rider or caregiver details, and transportation assistance needs. Please provide only the information needed to arrange transportation; do not send medical records, insurance identification numbers, or payment card details through this website.

How information is used

We use ride-request information to respond to inquiries, review availability, coordinate pickups and return trips, and communicate about requested services. If you choose to receive SMS messages, we may also send marketing and account notifications as described in our SMS Terms and Conditions.

SMS privacy

Privacy Policy Disclaimer: SMS Consent and phone numbers collected for SMS communication purposes will not be shared with any third party or affiliates for marketing purposes.

Service-related sharing

Information needed to fulfill a transportation request may be shared with drivers, dispatch staff, or service providers assisting with that request. Website hosting and communication providers may process information to deliver their services. We may disclose information when required by law. These service-related uses do not authorize third parties or affiliates to use SMS consent or phone numbers for their own marketing.

Website and email services

This website is hosted on Cloudflare and loads fonts from Google Fonts. Requests to these services can include technical information such as your IP address and browser information. Our current ride-request form opens your email application with your details filled in; you must send that email to submit the request. Your email provider processes messages according to its own policies. Links to other websites are subject to those websites’ privacy policies.

Your choices

SMS consent is optional and is not a condition of purchasing transportation. Reply STOP to opt out of SMS messages at any time. For SMS help, text 574-214-9226. You can contact safecare70@gmail.com to ask about information you provided or request correction or deletion. Requests may be subject to records we must retain for legal or service-related purposes.

Policy updates

Changes to this policy will be posted on this page with an updated date. For privacy questions, contact Safe Care Transportation at safecare70@gmail.com.
